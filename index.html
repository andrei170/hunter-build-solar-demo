Why commercial roofs are different

The usage matches the generation

A business uses most of its electricity in daylight, which is exactly when solar generates. That is not true of a house, where most of the use happens after dark.

  • Designed against your half-hourly usage, not a rule of thumb
  • Sized to what you consume, not to fill the roof
  • Battery only where it genuinely earns its place
The roof is already there

Large flat and shallow-pitch roofs on warehouses, barns and units are usually doing nothing else. No planning pressure, no aesthetic argument, no neighbours.

  • Structural check before we quote
  • Ballasted or fixed, depending on the build
  • Work phased around your operating hours
We will tell you if it is not worth it

Some roofs face the wrong way, some are near end of life, and some businesses simply do not use enough daytime power to justify it.

  • Survey first, quote second
  • Written assumptions, so you can challenge them
  • No pressure and no hard close
